WHEN RENEWABLE ENERGY IS NOT USED

The Effects of Fossil Fuel Dependence

Heavy dependence on fossil fuels can increase greenhouse gas emissions and create wider environmental and energy challenges.

UNDERSTANDING THE IMPACT

What Happens When We Depend on Fossil Fuels?

Burning coal, oil and natural gas provides energy but also produces greenhouse gases and other environmental pressures.

The IEA Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Energy database provides annual data on greenhouse gas emissions from energy, including CO₂, CH₄ and N₂O from fuel combustion as well as fugitive emissions from energy sectors.

VIEW IEA SOURCE
01

Higher Greenhouse Gas Emissions

When energy systems continue to depend heavily on fossil fuels, burning coal, oil and natural gas releases greenhouse gases. This contributes to the accumulation of heat-trapping gases in the atmosphere.

02

Increased Climate Pressure

Higher energy-related greenhouse gas emissions increase pressure on the global climate system and make climate targets more difficult to achieve.

03

Continued Fossil Fuel Dependence

Relying heavily on fossil fuels keeps energy systems dependent on coal, oil and natural gas instead of increasing the contribution of renewable sources.

04

Greater Environmental Pressure

Fossil-fuel-based energy production can create additional environmental pressures through fuel extraction, combustion and energy-related emissions.

05

Air Pollution

Burning fossil fuels can release pollutants alongside greenhouse gases, affecting air quality around energy generation, transportation and industrial activities.

06

Slower Energy Transition

Continued dependence on conventional energy sources can make the transition towards a lower-emission and more diversified energy system more challenging.

The Key Message

Renewable energy is not simply about producing electricity from different sources. It is part of a broader transition towards an energy system that can reduce greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on fossil fuels.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Why The Energy Transition Matters

Moving towards cleaner energy can help diversify energy systems, improve efficiency and reduce the environmental pressures associated with fossil fuel use.
