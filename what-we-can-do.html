Ways We Can Save Energy

Small changes in how we use electricity can help reduce energy wastage, lower electricity consumption and support a more sustainable future.

Save Energy At Home

Energy-saving practices at home can make electricity use more efficient while reducing unnecessary energy consumption.

TENAGA NASIONAL BERHAD

Energy Savings At Home

TNB provides energy efficiency tips to help households save electricity and use energy more efficiently. Its Energy Savings At Home resource includes practical tips, energy efficiency labels and information about household electricity usage.

VIEW TNB SOURCE
01

Power Down Unused Devices

Switch off electrical appliances and devices when they are not needed. Avoid leaving unnecessary equipment running.

02

Use Natural Light

Make use of natural daylight whenever possible instead of relying on artificial lighting during the day.

03

Use Air Conditioning Wisely

Use cooling appliances responsibly and avoid unnecessary operation. Keeping the cooling system efficient can help reduce electricity use.

04

Dry Clothes Naturally

Take advantage of sunlight to dry clothes naturally instead of relying on electrical drying equipment whenever practical.

05

Choose Efficient Appliances

Look at the energy efficiency label when choosing electrical appliances. TNB explains that the rating system ranges from 1 star to 5 stars, with 5 stars representing higher efficiency.

06

Monitor Electricity Usage

Monitor how much electricity your household appliances use and identify areas where energy can be used more efficiently.

More Stars. More Saving.

TNB explains that the energy efficiency label helps consumers understand the estimated energy consumption of electrical appliances. The rating ranges from 1 star to 5 stars, with 5 stars being the more efficient rating.
