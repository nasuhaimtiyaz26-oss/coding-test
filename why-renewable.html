WHY RENEWABLE ENERGY

Why Choose Renewable Energy?

Renewable energy provides cleaner, safer and more sustainable ways to power our world while reducing dependence on finite fossil fuels.

THE IMPORTANCE

Renewable Energy Creates A Better Future

Moving towards renewable energy can improve environmental protection, energy security, economic resilience and long-term sustainability.

01

Protects The Environment

Renewable energy produces little or no greenhouse gas emissions during operation, helping reduce pressure on the environment.

02

Improves Energy Security

Renewable resources such as sunlight, wind and water can reduce dependence on limited fossil fuel supplies.

03

Supports Economic Growth

The renewable energy sector creates opportunities for innovation, investment, infrastructure and new skills.

04

Builds A Sustainable Future

Renewable energy helps meet present energy needs while supporting a cleaner and more sustainable future for future generations.

ENERGY TRANSITION

Moving Towards Cleaner Energy

Energy transition means changing the way energy is produced and consumed by gradually replacing high-emission sources with cleaner and renewable alternatives.

Solar Energy

Example Of rooftop solar panels generating electricity from sunlight.

Wind Energy

Example Of wind turbines converting wind movement into electricity.

Hydropower

Example Of electricity generation using flowing or falling water.

Bioenergy

Example Of using organic materials such as agricultural residues for energy.

Energy Transition Starts With Change

A successful energy transition combines renewable generation, energy efficiency, better technology and responsible energy consumption.
