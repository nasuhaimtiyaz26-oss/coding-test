WHY IT MATTERS

Why Choose Renewable Energy?

Renewable energy can reduce dependence on fossil fuels while supporting a cleaner, more resilient energy system.

FOUR KEY BENEFITS

The Benefits of Renewable Energy

Renewable energy sources such as solar, wind and hydropower can support cleaner electricity generation and a more sustainable energy system.

01

Lower Emissions

Renewable sources such as solar and wind generate electricity without directly burning fossil fuels. This can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions associated with electricity generation.

02

Sustainable Resources

Renewable energy uses naturally replenished resources such as sunlight, wind and water. These resources can provide energy without relying entirely on finite fossil fuels.

03

Energy Security

A diversified energy system can reduce dependence on a limited range of fuel sources and support greater flexibility in meeting energy demand.

04

Protecting Our Future

Cleaner energy choices can contribute to long-term environmental protection and support efforts towards a more sustainable energy future.

CLEAN ENERGY SOURCES

Examples of Renewable Energy

Solar Energy

Energy from sunlight can be converted into electricity using solar photovoltaic systems.

Wind Energy

Wind turbines convert the movement of air into electrical energy.

Hydropower

Moving water can be used to generate electricity through hydropower systems.

Geothermal Energy

Heat from inside the Earth can be used as a source of renewable energy.

A Cleaner Energy Direction

Renewable energy is an important part of the transition towards a cleaner, more diversified and sustainable energy system.
