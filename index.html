02 / WHY IT MATTERS

Why safe energy?

A safer energy system is not only about producing power. It is about protecting the environment, communities and future generations.

🌱

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy can reduce pollution and lessen pressure on ecosystems and natural resources.

♻

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Replacing high-carbon energy sources with low-carbon alternatives can help address climate change.

⚡

Improves Energy Security

A diverse energy mix can make energy systems more resilient to supply disruptions.

🌍

Supports a Sustainable Future

Investing in cleaner technologies helps build an energy system for future generations.
