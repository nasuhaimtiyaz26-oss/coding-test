ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Effects of Relying on Fossil Energy

Continued dependence on fossil fuels contributes to greenhouse gas emissions and environmental pressures associated with energy production and use.

Higher Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Fossil fuel combustion is a major source of greenhouse gas emissions from the energy sector. Reducing fossil fuel dependence is therefore an important part of the transition toward lower-carbon energy systems.

Climate Pressure

Energy-related greenhouse gas emissions contribute to the accumulation of heat-trapping gases in the atmosphere, increasing pressure on climate systems.

Continued Fossil Fuel Dependence

Heavy dependence on fossil fuels can slow the transition toward cleaner and more diversified energy systems.

Environmental Pressure

Energy production and consumption can place pressure on air quality, ecosystems and natural resources, particularly when high-emission fuels dominate the energy system.

ENERGY EFFICIENCY

Ways to Save Energy

Practical energy-efficiency measures can reduce unnecessary energy use while maintaining comfort, productivity and safety.

01

Use Energy-Efficient Lighting

Replace inefficient lighting with energy-efficient alternatives and switch lights off when they are not required.

02

Manage Cooling Efficiently

Set cooling systems appropriately, maintain equipment and avoid unnecessary operation to reduce electricity consumption.

03

Reduce Standby Energy Use

Turn off equipment when it is not needed and avoid leaving unnecessary devices operating for extended periods.

04

Improve Home Energy Efficiency

Use efficient appliances, manage heating and cooling, and adopt smart energy practices throughout the home.

WHY IT MATTERS

Why Safe Energy?

Energy choices influence environmental quality, energy security and long-term sustainability.

01

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy systems can reduce environmental pressures and support the protection of ecosystems and natural resources.

02

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Greater use of low-carbon and renewable energy can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and help lower energy-related emissions.

03

Improves Energy Security

A diversified energy system can improve resilience by reducing excessive dependence on a single energy source.

04

Supports a Sustainable Future

Sustainable energy development can support long-term environmental, economic and social objectives.
