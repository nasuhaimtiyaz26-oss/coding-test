WHY IT MATTERS

Why Safe Energy Matters

A safer energy system protects people, nature and future generations.

01

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy can reduce environmental pressure and help protect ecosystems, forests and natural resources.

02

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Transitioning toward low-carbon energy can help reduce emissions associated with fossil fuel use.

03

Improves Energy Security

A diverse energy system can reduce dependence on a limited number of energy sources and improve resilience.

04

Supports a Sustainable Future

Investing in cleaner technologies can help create an energy system designed for long-term sustainability.
