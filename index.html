WHY IT MATTERS

Why Safe Energy?

Energy choices affect our environment, economy and future generations.

01

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy can reduce environmental damage and help protect ecosystems, forests and natural resources.

02

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Increasing renewable energy can reduce reliance on fossil fuels and help lower energy-related carbon emissions.

03

Improves Energy Security

A diverse energy system can reduce dependence on a single energy source and strengthen energy resilience.

04

Supports a Sustainable Future

Sustainable energy development can help create a cleaner and more resilient future for coming generations.
