WHY IT MATTERS

Why Safe Energy?

Energy choices affect our environment, economy and future generations.

01

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy can reduce environmental damage and help protect ecosystems, forests and natural resources.

02

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Increasing renewable energy can reduce reliance on fossil fuels and help lower energy-related carbon emissions.

03

Improves Energy Security

A diverse energy system can reduce dependence on a single energy source and strengthen energy resilience.

04

Supports a Sustainable Future

Sustainable energy development can help create a cleaner and more resilient future for coming generations.

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

The Effects of Continued Fossil Fuel Dependence

Energy-related greenhouse gas emissions are closely linked to the way societies produce and consume energy. Understanding these emissions is essential for evaluating the transition toward lower-emission energy systems.

IEA RESEARCH

Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Energy

IEA
CO₂ Carbon dioxide
CH₄ Methane
N₂O Nitrous oxide
GHG Energy-related emissions

The International Energy Agency's Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Energy database provides data and indicators related to greenhouse gas emissions associated with energy. It includes carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide emissions, supporting analysis of energy-sector environmental impacts.

VIEW IEA DATA SOURCE
01

Higher Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Continued reliance on carbon-intensive energy systems can result in higher energy-related greenhouse gas emissions, particularly from fossil fuel combustion.

02

Greater Climate Pressure

Greenhouse gases contribute to changes in the Earth's climate system. Reducing emissions from energy is therefore an important component of climate mitigation.

03

Continued Fossil Fuel Dependence

Continued dependence on coal, oil and natural gas can increase exposure to finite resources and changes in global energy markets.

04

Slower Energy Transition

Delaying the deployment of lower-emission technologies can make it more challenging to reduce the carbon intensity of energy systems over time.

Why the Energy Transition Matters

Increasing the deployment of renewable and other low-emission technologies can reduce dependence on carbon-intensive energy sources. The IEA's research highlights the important role of clean energy technologies in limiting the growth of energy-related emissions.

PRACTICAL ENERGY MANAGEMENT

Smarter Ways to Save Energy

Energy efficiency can be improved through smarter technology, better monitoring and more efficient management of household energy use.

ENERGY
STAR
OFFICIAL GUIDANCE

Smart Home Energy Management

ENERGY STAR's Smart Home guidance highlights connected technologies that can help households monitor, manage and reduce energy use. These approaches include smart thermostats, smart lighting, connected appliances and energy management systems.

VIEW ENERGY STAR GUIDANCE
01

Use Smart Thermostats

Smart thermostats can help manage heating and cooling schedules and allow users to adjust settings remotely according to household needs.

02

Manage Smart Lighting

Smart lighting systems can be scheduled and controlled remotely, helping users manage lighting according to occupancy and daily routines.

03

Monitor Energy Use

Monitoring electricity consumption can help identify usage patterns and reveal opportunities to reduce unnecessary energy consumption.

04

Automate Energy Management

Smart home energy management technologies can help schedule devices and coordinate energy use according to household routines.

05

Manage Connected Devices

Smart controls can help users manage connected devices so that equipment is operated according to actual household requirements.

06

Choose Efficient Products

ENERGY STAR certified products are designed to meet energy-efficiency requirements while providing the performance consumers expect.

Energy efficiency starts with better decisions.

Combining efficient products with smart controls and informed energy management can help households reduce unnecessary energy use while maintaining comfort and convenience.
