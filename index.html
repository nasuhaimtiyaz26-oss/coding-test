WHY IT MATTERS

Why Choose Renewable Energy?

Renewable energy can reduce dependence on fossil fuels while supporting a cleaner, more resilient energy system.

01

Lower Emissions

Renewable sources such as solar and wind generate electricity without directly burning fossil fuels.

02

Sustainable Resources

Renewable energy uses naturally replenished resources such as sunlight, wind and water.

03

Energy Security

A diversified energy system can reduce dependence on a limited range of fuel sources.

04

Protecting Our Future

Cleaner energy choices can contribute to long-term environmental protection and climate goals.

WHEN RENEWABLE ENERGY IS NOT USED

The Effects of Fossil Fuel Dependence

Heavy dependence on fossil fuels can increase greenhouse gas emissions and create wider environmental and energy challenges.

IEA RESEARCH

Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Energy

IEA
CO₂ Carbon dioxide emissions
CH₄ Methane emissions
N₂O Nitrous oxide emissions
GHG Energy-related greenhouse gases

The IEA Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Energy database provides annual data on greenhouse gas emissions from energy, including CO₂, CH₄ and N₂O from fuel combustion as well as fugitive emissions from energy sectors.

VIEW IEA SOURCE
01

Higher Greenhouse Gas Emissions

When energy systems continue to depend heavily on fossil fuels, burning coal, oil and natural gas releases greenhouse gases. This contributes to the accumulation of heat-trapping gases in the atmosphere.

02

Increased Climate Pressure

Higher energy-related greenhouse gas emissions increase pressure on the global climate system and make climate targets more difficult to achieve.

03

Continued Fossil Fuel Dependence

Relying heavily on fossil fuels keeps energy systems dependent on coal, oil and natural gas instead of increasing the contribution of renewable sources.

04

Greater Environmental Pressure

Fossil-fuel-based energy production can create additional environmental pressures through fuel extraction, combustion and energy-related emissions.

05

Air Pollution

Burning fossil fuels can release pollutants alongside greenhouse gases, affecting air quality around energy generation, transportation and industrial activities.

06

Slower Energy Transition

Continued dependence on conventional energy sources can make the transition towards a lower-emission and more diversified energy system more challenging.

The Key Message

Renewable energy is not simply about producing electricity from different sources. It is part of a broader transition towards an energy system that can reduce greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on fossil fuels.

Ways We Can Save Energy

Small changes in how we use energy can contribute to a more efficient and sustainable future.

ENERGY
STAR
ENERGY EFFICIENCY

Use Energy More Efficiently

Choosing efficient appliances, reducing unnecessary electricity use and improving energy efficiency can help reduce energy demand.

LEARN MORE
01

Turn Off Unused Devices

Switch off lights, computers and electrical devices when they are not needed.

02

Support Renewable Energy

Encourage the use of cleaner energy sources such as solar, wind and hydropower.

03

Reduce Energy Waste

Avoid unnecessary energy consumption and improve efficiency in homes, schools and workplaces.

04

Choose Efficient Transport

Walking, cycling, public transportation and efficient vehicles can help reduce energy consumption.

05

Improve Building Efficiency

Good insulation, efficient lighting and responsible cooling can reduce electricity demand.

06

Spread Awareness

Educating others about safe and sustainable energy can encourage better decisions across communities.

Every Energy Choice Matters

A cleaner energy future depends on better choices from individuals, organisations, industries and governments.
