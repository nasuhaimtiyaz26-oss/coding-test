WHY IT MATTERS

Why Safe Energy?

Energy choices influence environmental quality, energy security and long-term sustainability.

01

Protects the Environment

Cleaner energy systems can reduce environmental pressures and support the protection of ecosystems and natural resources.

02

Reduces Carbon Emissions

Replacing high-emission energy sources with low-carbon alternatives can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

03

Improves Energy Security

A diversified energy system can improve resilience by reducing dependence on a single energy source.

04

Supports a Sustainable Future

Sustainable energy development supports long-term environmental and economic resilience.

ENERGY & ENVIRONMENT

Effects of Continued Fossil Dependence

Energy-related greenhouse gas emissions are strongly connected to the continued use of fossil fuels.

Why the Energy Transition Matters

Fossil fuel combustion and related energy activities contribute significantly to greenhouse gas emissions. Without sufficient transition toward lower-emission energy systems, emissions remain a major environmental challenge.

IEA EMISSIONS DATA

Climate Pressure

Greenhouse gas emissions from energy contribute to climate change and increase pressure on efforts to decarbonise the energy system.

IEA • GHG EMISSIONS FROM ENERGY

Air Pollution

Energy systems based on combustion can release pollutants alongside greenhouse gases, creating additional environmental and public-health challenges.

ENERGY & ENVIRONMENT

Continued Emissions

Continued dependence on high-emission fuels makes the transition toward a lower-carbon energy system more difficult.

IEA DATA PRODUCT

Long-Term Environmental Risk

Persistent energy-related emissions increase the importance of cleaner generation, energy efficiency and responsible energy management.

Ways to Save Energy

Practical energy-management approaches based on ENERGY STAR Smart Home guidance.

01

Use Smart Thermostats

Smart thermostats can monitor and adjust temperature settings and can reduce heating or cooling when it is not required.

LEARN MORE
02

Improve Smart Lighting

Smart lighting can use schedules, sensors and remote controls to reduce unnecessary lighting when spaces are unoccupied.

LEARN MORE
03

Manage Devices Automatically

Smart home energy-management systems can schedule devices, suggest energy-saving actions and respond to occupancy.

LEARN MORE
04

Choose Efficient Appliances

ENERGY STAR certified appliances are designed to use energy efficiently while providing the required household function.

LEARN MORE

ENERGY efficiency reduces unnecessary energy demand, while renewable energy helps reduce dependence on high-emission energy sources. Both approaches are important components of a sustainable energy system.
